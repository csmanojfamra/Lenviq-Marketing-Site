# Claims and sources — the compliance cluster and the three state licensing pages

Seven pages, every regulatory claim each one makes, and the citation it gives for that claim. Where
a claim carries no citation, this says so. Nothing here is summarised and nothing is graded — the
point is the list, so that each line can be checked against the source beside it.

**These pages are live.** They were published on 9 October 2026 by commit `0e253ff`, whose message
records the decision: *"Manoj's call, and his to make — the brief reserved publishing these to him
and he has decided."* They were committed as drafts first (`7719f11` and the three state commits)
and the draft gate held until that commit opened it. So this is not a packet about a breach; it is
the claims inventory for pages already in circulation.

Read **§ Unverified by the pages themselves** at the end first. That is the short list the pages
flag in their own text, and it is the part that would cost something if wrong.
