RECITALS

A.Fastlegal is engaged in the business of developing and providing enterprise software, and has developed and owns "Lenviq", a loan origination and loan management platform for lending institutions, together with modules for accounting, management information, the assembly of data for regulatory returns and credit information reporting, and the generation of loan and related documents.

B.The Customer is a non-banking financial company registered with the Reserve Bank of India and carries on the business of lending. The Customer wishes to subscribe to Lenviq for use in its own lending business.

C.Fastlegal is a technology service provider only.
