An agent's round is what is due today. Their **book** is everything they are responsible for — and
the two are different questions. A borrower who rings up, walks over, or wants to pay early is not on
today's list.

## The bands are the lender's own

Regular, SMA-1, SMA-2, NPA — the same classification the desktop shows and the same one the
regulatory return reports. An agent saying "he's a bit late" to a manager reading SMA-2 is two people
having different conversations.

## Cash in hand

Undeposited cash is shown to the agent on their own screen and cleared when a branch confirms the
deposit. It is the figure they answer for at the end of the day.
